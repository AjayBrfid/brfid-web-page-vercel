Key Laws to Consider (India)

  1. Information Technology Act, 2000 (and IT Amendment Act, 2008) – covers data protection, cybercrime, intermediary liability.
  2. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) – governs collection, processing, and storage of personal data. Rules yet to be fully notified — monitor for updates.
  3. IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 – Grievance Officer appointment, 72-hour takedown compliance.
  4. GST Act – Invoice format and tax disclosure obligations.
  5. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 – Applicable if products/services are offered to individual consumers.
  6. Indian Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1933 – Relevant for RFID frequency and spectrum compliance (UHF 865–867 MHz band regulations in India).

Industry-Specific Considerations for RFID

  1. Hardware Warranty and RMA Policy – Define clear warranty terms, Return Merchandise Authorization process, and turnaround time for hardware issues.
  2. RFID Frequency Regulations – Ensure compliance with DoT (Department of Telecommunications) and WPC (Wireless Planning & Coordination) guidelines for RFID frequency usage in India.
  3. Site-Specific Deployments – Include provisions for site access, safety compliance, and environmental conditions for on-premise RFID installations.
  4. Tag Data Standards – Reference applicable standards (GS1 EPCglobal, ISO 18000 series) if relevant to the solutions provided.
  5. Service Level Agreement (SLA) – Define uptime guarantees, response times, and escalation matrix for the software platform.
  6. NDA / Confidentiality – If not handled in the Master Services Agreement (MSA), include a confidentiality clause.
  7. Intellectual Property – Clearly define ownership of custom integrations, RFID workflows, dashboards, and configurations developed for customers.
